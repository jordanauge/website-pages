mai 18, 2026

AAIF Accuracy & Reliability WG — charter approved

The charter of the Accuracy and Reliability working group in the AAIF Agentic AI Foundation (Linux Foundation) was completed and officially approved on May 18, 2026. The charter defines the WG scope, deliverable roadmap, and co-chairs (Jordan Augé / Casper Nielsen from Diagrid). First deliverable: terminology and landscape document covering resilience by design, recovery mechanisms, and evaluation for agentic AI.

juin 2026

AAIF Accuracy & Reliability WG — Terminology and Landscape first deliverable

First planned deliverable of the Accuracy and Reliability working group: a terminology and landscape document establishing shared vocabulary and mapping existing work on accuracy, reliability, resilience, and recovery for agentic AI systems. Currently in progress. Informed by active tech watch across the agentic ecosystem, covering resilience-by-design patterns, recovery mechanisms, memory architectures, and evaluation frameworks.
