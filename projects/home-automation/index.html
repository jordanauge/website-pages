Open home automation

This is a long term project to assemble an open and cloud-independent home automation system.

The underlying objective is not just convenience. I want a setup that remains understandable, locally controllable, and robust enough that I can keep it for years without being trapped by a vendor cloud or a brittle integration stack.

Tools and radio technologies

My home automation is currently centered around Home Assistant, mostly because I tested it first after reading a few reviews and it immediately gave me complete satisfaction.

Over time, I have tried Wi-Fi, 433 MHz, Bluetooth, and finally Zigbee devices, which I now tend to favor for both power efficiency and mesh capabilities, making it the only radio technology capable of covering my entire home. In addition, Zigbee devices are almost always directly usable in Home Assistant with ZHA, although I have not tried Zigbee2MQTT yet.

Wi-Fi still remains convenient for self-powered sensors, especially thanks to ESPHome, which allows quickly developing full-featured sensors, or for devices for which it is the only practical option.

433 MHz also remains useful for integrating older equipment, or when it is simply the only available option for devices I already own or for finding open devices. The main issue is the lack of smooth receiver integration and the need to decode proprietary frames quite often.
